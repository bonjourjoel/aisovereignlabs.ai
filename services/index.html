Services to Enterprises

AI Sovereign Labs provides hands-on intervention for complex software projects that have become difficult to understand, maintain, or complete. Every project is led directly by founder and software architect Joël Abenhaïm, with more than 20 years of software engineering experience.

Software Rescue & Legacy System Recovery
We take over software projects that have stalled and systems that have become fragile, poorly documented, or too risky to modify. This includes applications left without their original developers, interrupted projects, accumulated technical debt, and systems whose architecture or behavior is no longer fully understood.

We reconstruct the system, identify the root causes of failure, implement the required changes, restore maintainability, and produce the documentation needed for future development.
Large-Scale Modernization & Migration
We execute complex transformations across large codebases, including framework upgrades, language and runtime migrations, cross-language transpilation, and large-scale generation of tests or technical documentation.

We rely on proprietary analysis, automation, and verification tools to apply changes consistently across hundreds of files while keeping architectural control. From initial analysis through implementation and verification, we coordinate the final integration steps with the client's team when needed.
